BackupBell Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

The short version: BackupBell does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your personal information. Everything the app reads stays on your own phone and watch. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers involved.

Information the app uses

To do its job, BackupBell reads two things from your watch's sensors:

  • Heart rate — used to help detect whether you have fallen back asleep after your alarm.
  • Motion and activity data — used alongside heart rate to detect stillness consistent with sleep.

This sensor data is read only during an active monitoring window, which begins after your phone's alarm goes off and ends when the monitoring period expires or you turn the safety net off. The data is evaluated moment to moment on the watch itself and is not written to a file, saved to a history, or kept after the monitoring window ends.

Information the app stores

The only information BackupBell saves is your own settings: whether the safety net is switched on, and the length of the grace period you selected. These settings are stored locally on your devices and are synchronized between your paired phone and watch using Google Play Services, so that changing a setting on one device is reflected on the other.

Information the app sends

None. BackupBell does not request the Android INTERNET permission, which means the app is technically incapable of transmitting your information to us, to any server, or to any third party. There is no backend service behind this app.

Third parties

BackupBell contains no advertising networks, no analytics or crash reporting services, and no third-party tracking software of any kind. The app uses Google Play Services solely to exchange your settings and alarm signals between your own paired phone and watch. Your use of Google Play Services and Wear OS is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Body sensors — to read heart rate for sleep detection on the watch.
  • Physical activity — to detect stillness for sleep detection.
  • Notifications — to display the wake-up alert.
  • Full-screen intent — to show the alarm screen over the lock screen.
  • Vibration and wake lock — to alert you and wake the screen when the alarm fires.
  • Run at startup — to resume watching for your alarm after the device restarts.

Children's privacy

BackupBell is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. Because the app collects no personal information at all, none can be collected from children.

Your control over your data

You can revoke the sensor permissions at any time in your device's system settings, which stops the app from reading heart rate and motion data. Uninstalling BackupBell removes your saved settings from that device. Because nothing is transmitted or stored outside your devices, there is no account to close and no remote data to request or delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date above.

BackupBell

Your snooze safety net.

You set an alarm. You turned it off. You fell back asleep anyway.

BackupBell is the second line of defense behind the alarm you already use. Once you turn your alarm off, your watch quietly checks whether you actually got up. If you drift back off, it rings again on both your watch and your phone.

How it works

  • Keep using whatever alarm app you already have. Nothing changes.
  • When your alarm is turned off, BackupBell starts watching.
  • If you fall back asleep during the grace period you chose, it wakes you again.
  • Dismiss on either device and both go quiet.

Requirements

A Wear OS 3 watch or newer, paired with your phone. The watch does the sensing, so it needs to be on your wrist while you sleep.

Support

Questions, problems, or suggestions are welcome by email. See the privacy policy for what the app does and does not do with your data. The short version: it has no internet permission, so nothing leaves your devices.
